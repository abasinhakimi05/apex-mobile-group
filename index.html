Know Before You Buy or Sell

Our A–E grading standard

Every phone we buy or sell is inspected and assigned a condition grade, so you always know exactly what you're getting — or what your device is worth.

A

Excellent

Like-new condition, no visible scratches, fully functional, strong battery health.

B

Good

Light, minor wear that's barely noticeable in normal use. Fully functional.

C

Fair

Visible scratches or scuffs on the body or screen. Fully functional, great value.

D

Worn

Heavier cosmetic wear — dents, deeper scratches. Still fully functional.

E

Damaged / Parts

Cracked screen or functional issues. Sold as-is or for parts/repair.
